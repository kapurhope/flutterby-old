Module 1 of 6

Build the Best Bird Beak

Birds don’t have hands — their beaks are tools. Today you are the bird.

Hands-on 45–55 min Grades 5–8

Objectives

  • Collect “food” with an assigned beak under changing conditions.
  • Record data the class will reuse in Module 2.
  • Connect results to variation → environment → who does well.

Materials

Beaks (one per team): spoon, fork, chopsticks, clothespin, tweezers, cup

Foods: seeds (beans/rice), worms (rubber bands), soft fruit (pom-poms), floating food (paper clips or pom-poms in a shallow water tray)

Also: timer, score sheet (beak-score-sheet)

Instructions

“Birds don’t have hands. Their beaks are tools. Today you are the bird.”

Round A — Normal year (8 min)

  1. Assign each team one beak (fairness over choice).
  2. Give 30 seconds to collect as much as you can of one food type (or a mixed pile, then sort counts).
  3. Record counts on the score sheet.

Round B — Drought (6 min)

“Only large seeds remain.” Remove other foods; leave large beans only. Repeat 30 seconds. Record.

Round C — Flood (6 min)

“Most food is floating.” Use the water tray only. Repeat. Record.

Debrief (12–15 min)

  • Which beak won in each environment?
  • Did any beak win all rounds?
  • If flooding kept happening, which birds might struggle?

Introduce lightly: adaptation, variation, environment, trade-off.

Wrap (5 min)

Preview Module 2: “Tomorrow we teach a computer using your numbers.”

Differentiation & assessment

5th–6th: focus on “best tool for the job.” · 7th–8th: no beak wins everywhere — link to niche.

Assess: completed three-round score sheet + “Our beak was best when ___ because ___.”
