## Discussion prompts
1. What silly rule might an AI learn? (e.g., “needs a playground”)  
2. Why would that rule fail in Oak hills?  
3. How would you redesign the study?  

## Fix ideas (after students struggle)
- Sample **many regions**, not one favorite park.  
- Drop features that don’t make biological sense until tested.  
- Keep humans reviewing surprising predictions.
