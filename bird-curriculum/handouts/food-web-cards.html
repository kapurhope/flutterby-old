Food Web Cards

Instructions

  1. Print and cut out one card per student (duplicate insects/shrubs for larger classes).
  2. Stand in a circle. Start yarn at Sun, then connect who eats whom.
  3. Green-bordered cards are bird-focus — keep them in play.
  4. When the teacher announces a disruption, gently pull if you are connected.
