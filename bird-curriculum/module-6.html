Module 4 of 4 (core path) · Capstone

Protect the Flock

Azure Warblers need a plan. Limited money, real neighbors, messy data — live trade-offs on FlockQuest.

Computer-aided 50–70 min Debate with evidence

Open FlockQuest sim Scorecard handout

Objectives

  • Balance three goals: nest success, neighbor conflict, and budget.
  • Test at least two strategies and one stress event in FlockQuest.
  • Argue from evidence in a short mini-debate (cite 2–3 numbers).

Goals on the board (match the site)

  1. Nest success ≥ 75
  2. Neighbor conflict ≤ Medium
  3. Budget ≤ $100

Materials

  • Devices or stations with FlockQuest (projector for intro)
  • Optional print: capstone scorecard

No-device fallback: calculate the printed action menu by hand — same goals, slower feedback.

Instructions

“You have limited money, neighbors with opinions, and messy data. Hit the goals — or defend the trade-off.”

Intro (5 min)

Demo one preset + one event on the projector. Show Pass / Not yet badges.

Explore (20 min) — pairs

  1. Run Strategy A (try “Habitat first”).
  2. Save the run.
  3. Run Strategy B (try “People first” or invent your own).
  4. Test at least one event: heat wave, late freeze, or bad survey data.
  5. Save the second run.

Document (8 min)

Copy key numbers onto the scorecard (or keep the two saved runs on screen).

Mini-debate (12–15 min)

60–90 seconds each: best balance of the three goals. Class votes.

Reflect (5 min)

Cite 2–3 numbers + how an event changed the plan.

Differentiation & assessment

5th–6th: stick to presets; teacher leads one event. · 7th–8th: require “bad survey data” and explain why surveys matter.

Assess: two saved runs + reflection. Look-fors: uses data · names a trade-off · speaks clearly.
