Why teach AI literacy?

Black and Hispanic workers remain underrepresented in high-tech careers relative to their share of the U.S. workforce (about 7.4% and 9.9% of high-tech jobs vs 11.6% and 18.7% of workers — EEOC). Many adults still lack confidence with AI tools, even as AI reshapes school and work (Pew).

Through FlockQuest, our goal is to increase diversity and inclusion by teaching AI literacy early — with joyful bird ecology labs that sixth graders can run, move through, and remember. Students learn that a computer only follows the rules we give it, that bad data makes bad predictions, and that future scientists must keep humans in the loop.
