# Module 3 — Pull the String: Bird Food Web
**Time:** 40–50 min · **Mode:** Hands-on · **Supplies:** yarn, organism cards (`handouts/food-web-cards.md`)

## Hook (2 min)
“If one bird disappears, who feels it?”

## Goals
- Build a physical food web with a **bird focus**.  
- Feel cascade effects when something is removed.  
- Design a tiny resilience intervention ($100).

## Setup
Students in a circle; each gets a card (sun, plants, insects, mouse, frog, snake, songbird, hawk, owl, fungi, human, etc.). Extra students can duplicate insects/plants.

## Procedure

### Build (12 min)
1. Start yarn at **Sun**.  
2. Pass to anything that uses the sun’s energy (plants).  
3. Continue: who eats whom? Multiple connections OK.  
4. Ensure songbird/hawk/owl are well connected.

### Disruptions (15 min)
Announce one at a time; connected students **gently pull**:
1. “Drought kills half the grass/plants.”  
2. “Insecticide wipes out caterpillars.”  
3. “Hawks disappear.”  
4. Optional: “New houses remove nesting trees.”

After each: “Who felt that? What might happen next year?”

### Resilience challenge (12 min)
“Your conservation team has **$100**. Pick one action to make this web harder to break.”

Ideas: plant native shrubs ($40), reduce pesticide ($30), nest boxes ($20), cat collars / keep cats indoors campaign ($10)…

Groups share one plan and **who** in the web benefits.

## Wrap (5 min)
Link forward: “Tomorrow we predict *where* birds can live — another kind of pattern.”

## Assessment
Exit sticky: “If ___ is removed, ___ suffers because ___.”
