Module 3 of 6

Pull the String: Bird Food Web

If one bird disappears, who feels it? Students physically feel the web tug.

Hands-on 40–50 min Yarn + organism cards

Objectives

  • Build a physical food web with a bird focus.
  • Feel cascade effects when something is removed.
  • Design a tiny resilience intervention with a $100 budget.

Materials

  • Ball of yarn
  • Organism cards (sun, plants, insects, mouse, frog, snake, songbird, hawk, owl, fungi, human, outdoor cat…)
  • Open floor space for a circle

Instructions

“If one bird disappears, who feels it?”

Setup

Students stand in a circle; each gets a card. Duplicate insects/plants for larger classes.

Build the web (12 min)

  1. Start yarn at Sun.
  2. Pass to anything that uses the sun’s energy (plants).
  3. Continue: who eats whom? Multiple connections are OK.
  4. Make sure songbird, hawk, and owl are well connected.

Disruptions (15 min)

Announce one at a time; connected students gently pull:

  1. “Drought kills half the grass/plants.”
  2. “Insecticide wipes out caterpillars.”
  3. “Hawks disappear.”
  4. Optional: “New houses remove nesting trees.”

After each: “Who felt that? What might happen next year?”

Resilience challenge (12 min)

“Your conservation team has $100. Pick one action to make this web harder to break.”

  • Native shrubs — $40
  • Reduce pesticide — $30
  • Nest boxes — $20
  • Cats-indoors campaign — $10

Groups share who in the web benefits.

Wrap (5 min)

Preview Module 4: predicting where birds can live.

Assessment

Exit sticky: “If ___ is removed, ___ suffers because ___.”
